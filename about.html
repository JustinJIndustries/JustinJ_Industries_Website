About the studio

Websites and measurement, built together.

JustinJ Industries is an independent studio for hands-on web development and analytics implementation. We connect the visitor experience with the tracking, QA, and reporting that help a team understand what its site is doing.

Why the overlap matters

The page and the measurement plan should answer the same question.

Website decisions shape the data a team can trust. A form interaction, content path, or consent choice has to make sense to visitors before it can be measured well. Sensitive form contents stay out of analytics.

The work draws on custom front-end and CMS builds, GA4 and GTM tagging QA, and Looker Studio reporting. The page defines the interaction; testing checks that it is captured as intended; a usable report helps the team interpret it.

Working together

Close to the work, clear at handoff.

We can start with a page, workflow, or measurement question and define the scope together. The useful checks are agreed before implementation, so design, CMS behavior, tagging, and reporting can be reviewed against the same goal.

During the build, the working details stay visible. QA findings, changes, and open decisions are documented so the next person has a practical account of what was done and what still needs attention.
