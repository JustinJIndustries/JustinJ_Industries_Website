Good questions come first.

A few practical answers to help you prepare for a useful conversation.

What should I prepare for an initial conversation?

A short description of your business, the issue you want to address and your current reporting process is enough to begin. Share your preferred timing and the outcome you want to work towards. Detailed records can wait until a suitable scope and secure sharing method are agreed.

How is the scope agreed?

We start with the question, the information available and the people involved. A proposed scope should set out the work, responsibilities, useful outputs and review points before an engagement begins.

Can you work with an existing finance team or adviser?

The working approach can be shaped around the people already supporting your business. Clear ownership, agreed information requests and a shared review rhythm help keep the work connected.

What does tax planning support include?

It can include organizing year-end information, coordinating questions and preparing supporting schedules for your appointed tax advisers. Any tax filing, statutory assurance or regulated professional service must be separately scoped and confirmed with appropriately qualified professionals.

Do I need a new accounting system?

Not necessarily. The starting point is to understand the existing records, reporting process and practical needs. A system change should follow a clear reason and an agreed scope.

How do we begin?

Use the consultation page to outline your business context and area of interest. Focus on the question you want to answer; leave out sensitive account details and confidential financial records.
