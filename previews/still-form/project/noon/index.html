NOON

A sunny fictional citrus range, expressed through a packaging family, label hierarchy and campaign studies.

Project notes
Type
Packaging
Role
Concept direction, packaging graphics and campaign design
Purpose
Explore a citrus range that reads as one family while making each flavour easy to distinguish.
Scope
Three packaging-front studies; annotated label flat; landscape and portrait campaign applications
Status
Design study

A midday pause, made visible

NOON is an original packaging study for an imagined citrus refreshment. The brief is deliberately simple: make a small daily pause feel bright and direct, and build a range that can be recognised before someone reads every detail.

Family first, flavour second

A shared NOON wordmark and sun hold the family together. Lemon, Orange and Pomelo each get a distinct field colour and a written flavour name in the same place. The system avoids relying on colour alone, and the quieter supporting line leaves the product name room to lead.

From label to campaign

The annotated flat makes the hierarchy explicit: name, sun, flavour, then supporting detail. The landscape and portrait layouts test whether the same elements can move beyond a pack without becoming a different identity. The label and campaign graphics were created for this concept.

A graphic prototype, with clear limits

The deliverables explore visual hierarchy and applications. The illustrated 330 mL pack is a format study. It is not a tested container, production dieline or compliant food label; ingredients, legal information, material and print decisions remain outside this fictional brief.

Process notes

Each pack uses the same large wordmark, round sun and left-aligned flavour label. Colour helps separate the three variants, while written flavour names remain the primary cue. The campaign carries the same sun and typography into a landscape poster and a portrait social composition.
