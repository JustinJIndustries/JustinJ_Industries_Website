Golf, from above.

An illustrative aerial study of fairways, terrain and the relationship between land and play.

Visual description of this silent clip

Silent aerial footage moves forward over a tree-lined golf fairway. A curved path borders the grass, with buildings partly visible among the trees. There is no speech or other audible information.

THE STUDYBack to films ↗

A course has a rhythm that is difficult to see from the ground. This demo film study is shaped around the wider landscape, the route between holes and the details that give the place its character.

Frames from the film.
