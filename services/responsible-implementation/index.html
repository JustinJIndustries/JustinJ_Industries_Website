Services/Implementation approach

Agree what good looks like. Then test the work.

Accessibility, measurement and handoff requirements belong in the project scope and review record.

Accessible visitor journeys

  • Build with semantic HTML, readable content structure, keyboard access and visible focus.
  • Agree acceptance criteria, manual testing and third-party component review for the individual project.
  • Document review findings and ongoing content responsibilities.

Measurement with clear boundaries

  • Define event taxonomies around agreed business questions and implementation QA.
  • Keep names, email addresses and contact-form messages in contact systems and out of analytics event payloads.
  • Document tagging behavior, validation evidence and known limitations.

Accessibility conformance should be validated for each project’s scope, content, and platform.

Decide the review scope

Agree the platform, content, visitor journeys, review methods and handoff needs before implementation. Each project needs its own acceptance criteria.

Keep the record useful

Record what was tested, what remains outside scope and who maintains content and configuration after handoff.
