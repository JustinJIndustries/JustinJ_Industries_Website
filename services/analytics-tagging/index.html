Analytics & Tagging Implementation

Make measurement understandable and testable. JustinJ Industries helps connect website interactions to an agreed measurement plan, with clear event definitions and a record of what was validated.

Discuss analytics & tagging implementation

When this is useful

For teams preparing an implementation, troubleshooting inconsistent events, or reviewing whether existing reporting reflects the actions they actually need to understand.

What the work can include

  • A measurement plan with agreed events, parameters, and acceptance criteria.
  • GA4 and GTM configuration or a review of an existing implementation, according to scope.
  • Consent and data-handling considerations documented alongside the tagging design.
  • QA evidence, known limitations, and implementation or reporting handoff notes.

How we work

  1. Review business questions, the site, existing tags, consent behavior, and access requirements.
  2. Define events and exclusions before configuration, including what must never enter analytics.
  3. Validate agreed journeys, compare expected and observed behavior, and document the result.

Before we talk

Share the site URL, the questions reporting should answer, and the tools already in use. Access should be granted through the relevant platform; do not send account passwords or customer records.

Scope and limitations

Server-side tagging, Adobe Analytics or AEP support, Looker Studio dashboards, and complex integrations are scoped separately. A button click does not by itself establish a qualified lead, completed sale, or connected phone call.
