What the work can include

Define the deliverables before implementation begins.

For a scoped campaign launch, a review of an existing advertising setup, or a clearer connection between paid traffic and the actions reporting should measure.

Campaign plan
A campaign brief covering objectives, audience, placements, messaging, landing pages and review criteria.
Setup & review
Google Ads, Microsoft Advertising (Bing Ads) or Meta Ads campaign setup and review within the agreed account access, media budget and scope.
Journey & measurement checks
Landing-page and conversion-path checks, naming and campaign parameters, with tracking dependencies identified separately.
Optimization & reporting
Optimization notes and reporting in the relevant advertising platform or Data Studio, with definitions, limitations and next steps.

From first review to a documented release.

  1. Define the campaign

    Review the offer, audience, existing account structure and visitor journey. Agree on the objective, budget authority, access and success measures.

  2. Prepare & review

    Prepare the agreed campaign structure, messaging and landing-page handoff. Review links, tracking dependencies and approval checkpoints before launch.

  3. Measure & improve

    Review campaign and conversion data against agreed definitions. Document changes, reporting limitations and the next decisions.

Before we talk

Share the business objective, audience or service area, landing page and advertising platforms in use. Account access is arranged through platform permissions; do not send passwords or customer lists through the inquiry form.

Scope, clearly agreed

Account access, media budget, creative production and ongoing management require an agreed scope. Tracking and consent configuration have their own implementation scope.
