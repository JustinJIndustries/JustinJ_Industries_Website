Scope and outputs

What we can deliver

Website, analytics, or reporting work can be scoped for a public-sector team or prime contractor, with outputs agreed before implementation.1

Websites and content systems

Public-facing websites and program landing pages may need responsive builds, WordPress or Craft CMS work, navigation, technical SEO and schema, or accessibility remediation.

Depending on scope, outputs may include

  • Responsive templates, reusable content, and navigation
  • Metadata, accessibility QA, and editor handoff notes

Analytics, tagging, and dashboards

Measurement scopes can cover GA4/GTM or Adobe Analytics/AEP implementation and QA, Tags/Launch rules, Web SDK and Edge Network events, XDM and data layers, consent-aware or server-side tagging, and Looker Studio dashboards.

Depending on scope, outputs may include

  • Measurement plans, event definitions, and tagging QA findings
  • Dashboard views, change logs, and review notes

Delivery path

How work proceeds

  1. 01

    Define the need

    Identify the page, workflow, or measurement question. Agree on the work, outputs, and useful checks before implementation.

  2. 02

    Build and verify

    Implement the agreed changes, then review usability, tagging behavior, consent conditions, and relevant edge cases against the scope.

  3. 03

    Document the handoff

    Leave QA notes, change details, and practical guidance on what the team may need to maintain or decide next.
