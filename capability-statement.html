Capability statement

Government buyers & prime contractors

WEBSITES • MEASUREMENT • DIGITAL SUPPORT

JustinJ Industries is a Boston-based studio for websites, analytics implementation and practical digital support. We help teams scope useful changes, verify the work and maintain it after handoff.

Core services and outputs

Websites

Responsive builds and redesigns, WordPress/Craft CMS, technical SEO and accessibility remediation.

Outputs: Page templates, content patterns, metadata and editor notes.

Measurement

GA4/GTM and Adobe Analytics/AEP implementation, consent-aware tagging and dashboards.

Outputs: Measurement plans, event/data-layer definitions, QA findings and reporting views.

Digital support

Website/CMS maintenance, implementation reviews and practical technical documentation.

Outputs: Scoped fixes, change logs, test notes and maintenance handoffs.

Connected delivery. Documented checks.

  • Website and measurement decisions reviewed together.
  • WCAG-informed keyboard, semantic HTML and automated accessibility checks.
  • Privacy-aware implementation, clear QA findings and practical handoffs.

Accessibility conformance should be validated for each project’s scope, content and platform. Sensitive form contents are not sent to analytics.
