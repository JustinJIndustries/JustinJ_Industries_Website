Starting scopes

The sample pages show visual possibilities. The included work is defined by the package, then confirmed in writing for your project.

Static four-page site $950

Home, Services, About, Contact. HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; no CMS, backend, form, commerce, or blog. Contact uses email and phone links.

  • 14 calendar days after all mandatory inputs are received
  • Two revisions; up to five sections per page
  • Up to 2,000 client-provided words and 12 licensed client-supplied images, excluding a logo
  • One initial deployment to a compatible, client-ready static host
  • Seven-day defect-fix window

Domain or DNS migration and server repair are outside this scope. Optional updates are $70/hour with a $70 minimum per request, agreed beforehand.

Simple WordPress site $1,999

Home, Services, About, FAQ, Contact, using a simple WordPress theme approach. Includes a standard contact form, basic antispam, and simple metadata.

  • 28 calendar days after all mandatory inputs are received
  • Two revisions; up to five sections per page
  • Up to 3,000 client-provided words, 20 licensed client-supplied images, and 10 FAQs
  • 30-minute handoff and seven-day defect-fix window

Hosting, domains, paid tools, stock assets, and renewals are client-paid and agreed separately. Clients must have rights to supplied content.

Still Form and AER/FIELD are custom-scope examples; their advanced functionality is not included in the $1,999 WordPress package. Direct clients pay by check after an agreed scope. Projects originating on Upwork remain on Upwork.
