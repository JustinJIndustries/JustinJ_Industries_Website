How we work

Clear scope. Careful review. Usable handoff.

Practical checks and clear notes keep the work useful after launch.

  1. 01

    Scope the work

    Agree on the pages, workflows, or measurement questions and what the result should show.

  2. 02

    Build and review

    Implement the agreed work, then check usability, accessibility, tagging behavior, and privacy where the scope calls for them.

  3. 03

    Hand over clearly

    Document what changed, what was checked, and what the team needs to maintain.
