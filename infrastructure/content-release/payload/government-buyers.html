Scope and outputs

What we can deliver

Website, digital advertising, measurement or support work can be scoped for a public-sector team or prime contractor, with outputs agreed before implementation.1

Websites

Responsive websites and program landing pages, WordPress or Craft CMS, technical SEO and schema, and accessibility remediation.

Depending on scope, outputs may include

  • Responsive templates, reusable content, and navigation
  • Metadata, accessibility QA, and editor handoff notes

Measurement

GA4/GTM implementation; Adobe Analytics and Adobe Experience Platform Tags (Adobe Launch) specifications and QA. Consent-aware tagging, data-layer review and reporting are agreed with the scope.

Depending on scope, outputs may include

  • Measurement plans, event definitions, and tagging QA findings
  • Adobe Analytics Analysis Workspace or Data Studio reporting, change logs, and review notes

Digital support

Website/CMS maintenance, implementation reviews and practical technical documentation.

Depending on scope, outputs may include

  • Scoped fixes, change logs and test notes
  • Editor guidance and practical maintenance handoffs

Digital advertising

Scoped paid search, display and paid social support for outreach or awareness campaigns. Agree on audience, placements, landing-page handoff and measurement before launch.

Review advertising support

Depending on scope, outputs may include

  • Channel plans, search/ad-copy reviews and display/social creative variants
  • Google Ads, Microsoft Advertising (Bing Ads) and Meta Ads, as scoped
  • Placement and budget change logs, reporting definitions and handoff notes

Delivery path

How work proceeds

  1. 01

    Define the need

    Identify the page, workflow, or measurement question. Agree on the work, outputs, and useful checks before implementation.

  2. 02

    Build and verify

    Implement the agreed changes, then review usability, tagging behavior, consent conditions, and relevant edge cases against the scope.

  3. 03

    Document the handoff

    Leave QA notes, change details, and practical guidance on what the team may need to maintain or decide next.
