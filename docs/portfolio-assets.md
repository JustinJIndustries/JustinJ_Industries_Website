## Official WordPress W mark

The unmodified transparent gray and white PNGs are from the W Mark downloads on
[WordPress.org Graphics & Logos](https://wordpress.org/about/logos/):
`https://s.w.org/style/images/about/WordPress-logotype-wmark.png?0=` and
`https://s.w.org/style/images/about/WordPress-logotype-wmark-white.png`.
Both originals are 1000 × 1000 px and are displayed at 27 × 27 px without
recoloring or distortion. Gray is used on light cards; white is used on dark cards.

The [WordPress Foundation trademark policy](https://wordpressfoundation.org/trademark-policy/)
was reviewed on 2026-10-17. The mark is used only to identify WordPress in a
descriptive service card, with a contextual attribution reference and a
non-affiliation/non-endorsement notice above the footer. It is not JJI branding
or an advertisement asset. Attribution is not a copyright release, an assertion
of a license grant, or a claim of affiliation. The adjacent heading names the
service, so the duplicate logo image is decorative for assistive technology.
